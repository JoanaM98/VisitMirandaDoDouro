# Miranda do Douro — Guia Turístico

An informational tourism website dedicated to Miranda do Douro and its municipality, created to help guests discover the region while also showcasing the destination to prospective visitors.

The website is designed to be shared with guests staying at our rural tourism units, Casa do Poço and Casa do Margalho, while also serving as a digital guide to the area's culture, gastronomy, nature, traditions and local experiences.

## Features

- **History** — Discover the history and heritage of Miranda do Douro.
- **Culture** — Explore local traditions, the Mirandese language, Pauliteiros de Miranda and the Capa de Honras Mirandesa.
- **Gastronomy** — Discover traditional dishes.
- **Restaurants** — Recommendations for places to eat in the municipality.
- **Local Breeds** — Information about the region's native breeds, including the Mirandese Donkey, Mirandese cattle and Mirandese Sheep.
- **Activities** — Suggestions for experiences and things to do in the region.
- **Viewpoint Route** — Explore some of the most impressive viewpoints around Miranda do Douro.
- **Events Calendar** — A calendar featuring festivals, fairs, religious celebrations and other local events.
- **Map** — Interactive map with relevant places and points of interest.
- **Day Getaways** — Suggestions for destinations that can be visited on a day trip from Miranda do Douro.
- **Accommodation** — Information about our two rural tourism units:
  - Casa do Poço
  - Casa do Margalho

## Purpose

The website has two main purposes:

1. **Guest guide** — Provide guests staying at Casa do Poço and Casa do Margalho with useful information and inspiration to make the most of their visit.
2. **Tourism showcase** — Present Miranda do Douro and its surrounding municipality to potential visitors and encourage longer stays in the region, while introducing our accommodation options.
